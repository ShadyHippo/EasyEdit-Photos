Easy Edit
Annotate
Drop an image, paste with Ctrl+V, or tap Open
Pick a drawing or effect tool to see its options here.

Style

Magic wand

Tap an area to replace it. Transparent clears it to see-through; untick to fill it with the Color here. Raise tolerance to match more shades at once.

Crop

Drag the handles to resize, drag inside to move.

Blob & fry

Tap to add a blob, drag it to move. Blobs bulge the picture before frying, and are applied to what frying targets (the selected layer, or the background). UI only, never drawn into the image.

Annotations

Left to right is back to front (rightmost draws on top). Drag a chip to restack it. Ctrl-click toggles one, Shift-click picks a range — same as on the canvas.

Layers

No layers yet
Ticked rows are drawn into the background in their current order (undo reverts it). Ctrl/Shift-click rows to select several, then drag them together in the canvas.

Selection

Whole image

Adjust

Affects the final image, or just the selection if you have one. Apply bakes it in as its own layer.

Effects

Works on what you see: the selection is flattened and the effect applied to it, then kept as its own layer. No selection = the whole picture. Ctrl+A selects all.

Remove background

Applies to the selected layer, or the background. Uses your rembg container; the Wand does it instantly on-device.

Resize

Locked: the other side follows as you type. Unlocked: distort freely - every layer, annotation and text scales with it.
